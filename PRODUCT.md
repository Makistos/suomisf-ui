## Platform

web

## Users

Primary, in priority order when needs conflict:

1. **Admin editors**: the small team that adds and corrects works, editions, people, short stories, magazines, covers and award data. Data entry and correction is a core, daily workflow, not an afterthought.
2. **Bibliographers**: look up exact publication history: which edition and printing (laitos/painos) appeared when, from which publisher, in which series, with which translator and cover artist.

Secondary:

3. **Collectors**: track the editions they own or want, with condition and second-hand prices.
4. **Researchers**: study Finnish SF publishing, short-story appearances, fanzines, awards and statistics.
5. **Readers**: find what to read next through authors, series, awards, tags and suggestions, and mark works read.

## Product Purpose

SuomiSF (sf-bibliografia.fi) is the bibliography of Finnish-language speculative fiction: books, editions and printings, short stories, magazines and fanzines, people, publishers, book series, publisher series, awards and non-fiction about SF. It exists to be the most complete and precise record of Finnish SF publishing. Success means a bibliographer or collector finds the exact edition-level answer here, and finds it here rather than anywhere else.

## Positioning

- **Edition-level completeness**: every edition and printing, with binding, dust jacket and cover detail, not just library holdings.
- **Combined sources**: merges the original book catalogue with the National Library (Kansalliskirjasto), Risingshadow, Kirjasampo, other sources and the maintainers' own copies, so it holds more about works, editions and people than any single source.
- **SF-specific**, unlike general catalogues such as Finna and Kirjasampo.
- **Cross-referencing** between works, editions, people, short stories, series, publishers and awards that no other site matches.
- **Best support for book series and publisher series.**
- **Unique coverage**:
  - the most comprehensive listing of Finnish SF fanzines
  - an unparalleled short-story collection
  - awarded books listed with precision
  - non-fiction about SF listed clearly
  - publisher and person pages more detailed than anywhere else
- **More comprehensive search tools** than other sites.
- **Statistics**: still a work in progress, but already more than others offer.

## Operating Context

- **Public browsing** needs no account: works, editions, people, short stories, magazines and issues, publishers, series, awards, tags, latest additions, statistics, and the book and short-story indexes.
- **Logged-in users** keep a personal collection (owned editions with condition, wishlist), read status with an opinion, book suggestions, collection statistics and second-hand price tracking from several Finnish second-hand bookshops.
- **Admins** create and edit every entity through forms on the entity pages, upload and pick covers and person images, link short stories to works and issues, import award winners, and review a change log.
- **Errors** are reported by email to yp (at) sf-bibliografia.fi.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The interface and content are in Finnish.
- Domain terminology is precise and must be kept, as defined in the site's FAQ:
  - **laitos** (version) vs **painos** (printing)
  - **sidottu** (hardcover) vs **nidottu** (paperback)
  - **ylivetokansi** (printed hardcover) vs **kansipaperi** (dust jacket)
- No other product constraints were set at the time of writing.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **SuomiSF**; domain sf-bibliografia.fi.
- The code is open source.

## Evidence on Hand

- **Data sources:** the original book catalogue, Kansalliskirjasto (ISBN and base data), Risingshadow (descriptions and covers), Kirjasampo (subject tags), Antikvaari (covers) and covers scanned from the maintainers' own books.
- **Person images:** from Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and Wikidata, reuse-licensed only, with licence and author shown.
- **Absences:** there are no testimonials, user counts or press quotes on hand. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. **Precision over approximation.** Edition-level facts are the product. Where data is uncertain, say so; never smooth it over.
2. **The editor's workflow is first-class.** Entering and correcting data must be fast and reliable, because completeness depends on it.
3. **Everything links.** Every work, edition, person, story, series, publisher and award should lead to its related entities.
4. **SF-specific depth beats general breadth.** Serve the specialist (the bibliographer, the collector) before the casual visitor.
5. **Respect the sources.** Show provenance and licences where they matter (covers, person images).
